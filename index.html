About

Mediocre racing driver and Porsche enthusiast. I get along with people who have taste.

Taught myself design and code. Got a job on the other side of the world. Moved. Spent three years witnessing one of the craziest periods of hypergrowth in tech. Then Covid hit. Co-founded a company, it was a lot of fun. Moved again — to London. Now I help early-stage companies with funding and connect them with good friends from the industry. Obsessed with flat-six cars and driving them on a 20.832 km track in Germany.
